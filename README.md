# LinguaQuest 🌍
### Interactive Beginner Polyglot Learning Platform
**Featuring Hausa, German, Latin, Modern Greek, and French**

---

## 🌟 Overview
**LinguaQuest** is an interactive, gamified language learning web platform designed specifically for beginners. It features a complete learning curriculum across five diverse and influential world languages:

1. 🇳🇬 **Hausa (Harshen Hausa)**: The major West African lingua franca, with interactive phonetic breakdowns of hooked letters (`ɓ`, `ɗ`, `ƙ`, `'y`, `ts`), tone systems, and market bargaining etiquette in Kano.
2. 🇩🇪 **German (Deutsch)**: Master umlauts (`ä`, `ö`, `ü`, `ß`), the golden rule of capitalizing all nouns, gender articles (`der`, `die`, `das`), formal `Sie` vs casual `du`, and café ordering in Berlin.
3. 🏛️ **Latin (Lingua Latina)**: Restored Classical pronunciation (hard `C` always as `/k/`, `V` as `/w/`), famous historical mottoes (*Carpe diem*, *Vēnī vīdī vīcī*), roots of English vocabulary, and dialogues in the Roman Forum.
4. 🇬🇷 **Modern Greek (Ελληνικά)**: The complete 24-letter Greek Alphabet Studio with audio soundboard, the "Ne = Yes" vs "Ochi = No" trap, ordering souvlaki in Athens, and Greek roots of science & philosophy.
5. 🇫🇷 **French (Français)**: Accent guides (`é`, `è`, `ê`, `ç`), nasal vowels, silent final consonants, liaison rules, polite café etiquette, and morning dialogues in Paris.

---

## 🎮 Interactive Learning Modes
- 🎴 **3D Flashcards Studio**: Flip cards with native audio, phonetic guides, IPA, example sentences, and "Got It!" vs "Still Learning" progression.
- 🎯 **Quiz Arena**: Multiple choice quizzes with instant explanations, plus **Sentence Scramble / Word Tile Puzzles** where you click and assemble authentic sentences.
- ⚡ **Speed Match Mini-Game**: Match 5 native terms with their English translations against a live stopwatch with combo multipliers and high scores.
- 🔤 **Alphabet & Sounds Studio**: Interactive soundboard for every letter and special character (including hooked letters `ɓ`, `ɗ`, `ƙ` and Greek alphabet `α..ω`), complete with pronunciation rules.
- 📖 **Phrasebook & Grammar Guide**: Searchable categorized dictionary with instant audio playback, and bite-sized grammar cards (genders, word orders, politeness).
- 💬 **Immersion Dialogues**: Real-world conversations (at the Kano market, Berlin bakery, Roman forum, Athenian taverna, and Parisian café) with line-by-line audio and **Roleplay Practice Mode**.

---

## 🔊 Audio & Gamification
- **Native Web Speech TTS Engine**: Immediate audio pronunciation for Greek, German, French, Latin, and Hausa.
- **Synthesized Audio Effects (Web Audio API)**: Zero-external-dependency sound effects for correct answers, errors, card flips, and milestone fanfare.
- **Canvas Confetti**: Celebratory bursts when completing quizzes and decks.
- **XP, Daily Streaks & Badges**: Persisted in `localStorage`, complete with rank titles from *Language Novice* to *Polyglot Master*.
- **Dark / Light Mode**: Beautiful modern UI styled with custom themes per language.
